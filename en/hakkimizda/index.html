About us

Nokta Haber is a fictional demo portal that verifies local news with two sources and publishes it with dates. Stories, people and organisations are fictional; no official notices are published.

The first-publication date is stated unchanged on the story; corrections and updates are dated separately. Ownership and editor details are on the Masthead page.
